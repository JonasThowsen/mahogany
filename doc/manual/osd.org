* Volume, Brightness and the On-screen Display

The volume and brightness keys of the keyboard (XF86AudioRaiseVolume
and friends) are bound outside any prefix to the commands =volume-up=,
=volume-down=, =volume-mute=, =microphone-mute=, =brightness-up= and
=brightness-down=. They go through =wpctl= (WirePlumber) and
=brightnessctl=, in steps of =mh::*volume-step*= and
=mh::*brightness-step*= percent (5), and show the new value for a
moment in the on-screen display, a small panel with a bar.

=mh::*osd-timeout*= (1500 ms), =mh::*osd-position*= (=:top=, =:center=
or =:bottom=) and =mh::*osd-theme*= configure the display, and
=(mh::osd-show "Caps lock" :icon "⇪")= shows anything else on it:
=:percent= adds the bar.

The backlight is the integrated GPU's when there are several
(=intel_backlight= or =amdgpu_bl*= before others, NVIDIA's last): on a
laptop with two GPUs the panel hangs off the integrated one and the
other backlight changes nothing. =(setf *backlight-device* "nvidia_0")=
names one by hand; =brightnessctl -l= lists them.
