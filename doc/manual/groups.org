* Groups

Groups are Mahogany's workspaces, and each output has its own, the way
dwm's tags and Hyprland's workspaces are per monitor: an output shows
one of its groups, numbered 1, 2, 3 …, and the keys act on the output
that has the focus. =s-2= switches the focused output to its group 2,
made when it is missing, and leaves the other outputs as they are;
=s-C-2= sends the focused window to group 2 of its output. =gnext= and
=gprev= (=s-d= / =s-u=) go through the focused output's groups by
number, around the end.

The current group is the group of the focused frame. Focusing a frame
on another output, by direction (=s-l=), with =s-S-l= / =s-S-h=, or by
clicking, makes the group shown there the current one, so the mode-line
of each output marks the group that output shows. A window goes to
another output by moving into the group shown there, with =s-C-S-l=
and the like, =move-window-to-next-output=, or the output menu; it
leaves its group for that one.

A group is named by its number unless =grename= gives it a name;
=*group-layout-defaults*= and the like go by name, so ="2"= there means
group 2 of every output.

** Outputs coming and going

When an output goes away (unplugged, or the lid closes, see
[[file:touchpad.org][Touchpad, Mouse and Keyboard Locks]]), its groups move with their
windows to the first output left, as niri's workspaces do: there they
take the numbers free after that output's own groups (its 1, 2 become
3, 4 on an output that has two), and the group that was current is
shown. They remember their own output, and go back to it, with the
windows they have then, when it returns; the output that hosted them
shows its own first group again. Without another output the groups
wait, their windows hidden, and the next output to appear takes them
over. A new output starts with a group 1 of its own.

A group named by its number is renamed as it moves; one named with
=grename= keeps its name.

** From Lisp

- =(output-groups state output)= :: the groups of OUTPUT, by number.
- =(state-output-group state output)= :: the group OUTPUT shows.
- =(state-current-group state)= and its =setf= :: the current group;
  setting it shows the group on its output and focuses it.
- =(mahogany-state-group-add state &key group-name number home
  make-current)= :: a new group on HOME (an output, its full name, or
  the focused output), or =:none= for one never shown, as the
  scratchpad's.
- =(mahogany-state-group-remove state group)= :: removes it, its
  windows going to the group before it; the only group of an output
  stays.
- =(mahogany-group-origin group)= :: the output a group away from
  home goes back to, NIL at home.
- =(move-view-to-frame view frame &key follow swap)= :: a window into a
  frame, of its own group or of the group shown on another output.
