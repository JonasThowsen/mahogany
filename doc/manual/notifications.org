* Notifications

Mahogany is the notification service of the session
(=org.freedesktop.Notifications= on the session bus, through
libsystemd's sd-bus, read from the compositor's own event loop), so
=notify-send= and every program that notifies works without mako or
dunst. A notification is a card at a corner of the current output,
with the summary, the body and the program's name; critical ones have
a red edge and stay until dismissed, the others go after their time
or =*notification-timeout*=. A click dismisses a card.

- =notification-dismiss= (=s-n=): the newest one goes.
- =notification-dismiss-all= (=s-S-n=).
- =notifications-toggle= (=s-C-n=): do not disturb; notifications go to
  the history only.
- =notification-history= (=s-M-n=): the launcher lists what came in;
  choosing one shows it again until dismissed.

#+BEGIN_SRC lisp
  (setf *notification-position* :bottom-right)   ; :top-right by default
  (setf *notification-timeout* 8000)
  (setf *notification-width* 420)
  (setf *notification-max-shown* 3)
#+END_SRC

=*notification-hooks*= holds functions called with each notification
as it comes in; one returning =:hide= keeps it off the screen:

#+BEGIN_SRC lisp
  (push (lambda (n) (when (string= (notification-app n) "Spotify") :hide))
        *notification-hooks*)
#+END_SRC

Markup in bodies is stripped; actions are received but not shown yet.
Another notification daemon running when Mahogany starts keeps the
name, and Mahogany says so in its log.
